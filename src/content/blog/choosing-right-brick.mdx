Picking the wrong brick can slow your build and raise costs. Here is a simple breakdown.

## Solid bricks

Use for **load-bearing walls** and foundations where strength matters most.

## Hollow bricks

Lighter and better insulated — ideal for **fast, energy-efficient** construction.

## Paving & interlocking

Best for **driveways, compounds and pathways** where a clean, durable surface is needed.
