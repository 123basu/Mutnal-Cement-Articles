Traditional clay bricks are fired in kilns at high temperatures, consuming large amounts of
coal and topsoil. **Fly-ash cement bricks** take a different path.

## What is fly-ash?

Fly-ash is a fine byproduct of coal-based power generation. Instead of sending it to
landfills, we blend it with cement and aggregates to form durable, dimensionally consistent
bricks.

## Key benefits

- Lower carbon footprint per brick
- Higher strength and uniform size
- Better thermal insulation for buildings
- Reduced wastage on site

> Switching to fly-ash bricks is one of the simplest ways a builder can lower a project's
> environmental impact.
